ABOUT US

ACE Precision Technology Co., Ltd. (ACE) started out from manufacturing watch casing & belt.

In recent years, the company expanded the operations with sub-contracted manufacturers in Taiwan and earned endorsements from major customers in the US and Europe. With its successful efforts in Fully Machined Components, Castings, Extrusions, Forgings, Stampings, Secondary Processing and Value-Added Assembly, it has become a full service manufacturing provider for golf product, watch parts, medical devices, and high-precision 3C components.
